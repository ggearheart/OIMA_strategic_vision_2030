Office of Information Management & Analysis

Strategic Vision 2030

Turning California's water data into trusted, accessible information that protects public health and the environment.

Vision Pillars Goals Roadmap

Our Vision

By 2030, OIMA is the connective tissue for water information across the state — making high-quality data open by default, easy to find, and ready to drive decisions from the watershed to the boardroom.

Mission

Steward the data and information systems that the Water Boards rely on to protect water quality and water rights.

Values

Transparency, equity, scientific integrity, and service to the communities we protect.

Strategic Pillars

01

Open & Accessible Data

Publish authoritative water data openly, with the tools and context people need to use it.

02

Modern Infrastructure

Build resilient, cloud-ready platforms that scale with the state's data and security needs.

03

Data-Driven Decisions

Equip programs and the public with analytics, visualization, and AI that turn data into insight.

04

People & Culture

Grow a skilled, collaborative data workforce and a culture of evidence-based practice.

Goals to 2030

  • Access 100% of priority datasets discoverable through a single open data catalog.
  • Quality Standardized data quality and metadata across core programs.
  • Speed Reduce time from data collection to public availability by half.
  • Insight Self-service dashboards for every major water program.
  • Trust Clear governance, privacy, and security for all managed data.

Roadmap

2026 — Foundation

Establish governance, inventory data assets, and modernize core platforms.

2027–2028 — Acceleration

Scale open data publishing, analytics, and shared services across programs.

2029–2030 — Realization

Embed data-driven decision making and measure impact against our 2030 goals.
