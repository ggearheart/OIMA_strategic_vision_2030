Office of Information Management & Analysis

Strategic Vision 2030

Turning California's water data into trusted, accessible information that protects public health and the environment.

Vision Pillars Goals Roadmap

Our Vision

By 2030, OIMA is the connective tissue for water information across the state — making high-quality data open by default, easy to find, and ready to drive decisions from the watershed to the boardroom.

Mission

Empower and support people, so that our water data systems educate communities and inform decisions to protect the environment and public health.

Our Values

We build trust

We strive to be a trusted source of water data, information, and processes, and we invest in building relationships.

We adapt

We stay flexible and innovative in how we work and in the products, services, and resources we develop.

We work in partnership

Collaboration and partnerships are key to who we are and how we work.

We drive quality

We are committed to producing quality information, data, products, and services.

Strategic Pillars

01

Modern Data Infrastructure

Build resilient, cloud-ready platforms that scale with the state's data and security needs — the foundation that delivers two ultimate endpoints:

Open & Accessible Data

Publish authoritative water data openly, with the tools and context people need to use it.

Use Data for All Key Decisions

Equip programs and the public with the analytics, visualization, and AI to ground every key decision in data.

02

Water Quality Monitoring

Sustain and integrate statewide monitoring — anchored by the Surface Water Ambient Monitoring Program (SWAMP) and coordinated through the California Water Quality Monitoring Council and its My Water Quality portal — so the data flowing into our infrastructure is trusted at the source.

03

People & Culture

Grow a skilled, collaborative data workforce and a culture of evidence-based practice.

Goals to 2030

  • Access 100% of priority datasets discoverable through a single open data catalog.
  • Quality Standardized data quality and metadata across core programs.
  • Speed Reduce time from data collection to public availability by half.
  • Insight Self-service dashboards for every major water program.
  • Trust Clear governance, privacy, and security for all managed data.

Roadmap

2026 — Foundation

Establish governance, inventory data assets, and modernize core platforms.

2027–2028 — Acceleration

Scale open data publishing, analytics, and shared services across programs.

2029–2030 — Realization

Embed data-driven decision making and measure impact against our 2030 goals.
