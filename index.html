Strategic Vision 2030

Turning California's water data into trusted, accessible information that protects public health and the environment.

Vision Pillars SWAMP Goals Roadmap How We Deliver

Our Vision

By 2030, OIMA delivers a modern data infrastructure whose two endpoints — water data that is open and accessible, and data that informs every key decision — are fed by trusted statewide monitoring and powered by a skilled, collaborative data workforce.

Mission

Empower and support people, so that our water data systems educate communities and inform decisions to protect the environment and public health.

Our Values

We build trust

We strive to be a trusted source of water data, information, and processes, and we invest in building relationships.

We adapt

We stay flexible and innovative in how we work and in the products, services, and resources we develop.

We work in partnership

Collaboration and partnerships are key to who we are and how we work.

We drive quality

We are committed to producing quality information, data, products, and services.

Strategic Pillars

01

Modern Data Infrastructure

Build resilient, cloud-ready platforms that scale with the state's data and security needs — the foundation that delivers two ultimate endpoints:

Open & Accessible Data

Publish authoritative water data openly, with the tools and context people need to use it.

Use Data for All Key Decisions

Equip programs and the public with the analytics, visualization, and AI to ground every key decision in data.

02

Water Quality Monitoring

Sustain and integrate statewide monitoring — anchored by the Surface Water Ambient Monitoring Program (SWAMP) and coordinated through the California Water Quality Monitoring Council and its My Water Quality portal — so the data flowing into our infrastructure is trusted at the source.

This extends to emerging strategies for new contaminants, including the California Microplastics Monitoring Network — whose standardized protocols, harmonized labs, and open data portal share this vision's 2030 horizon.

03

People & Culture

Grow a skilled, collaborative data workforce and a culture of evidence-based practice.

The SWAMP Golden Circle

The Surface Water Ambient Monitoring Program articulates its own purpose and practices — designed to nest within OIMA's Golden Circle and give the Water Quality Monitoring pillar its foundation.

Our WHY We are here to follow through on our commitments with compassion, so that we can build capacity and partnerships through our SWAMP programs.

Our HOWs

01

02

03

04

Use SWAMP data and science to support accountability and decision making.

05

Establish clear priorities and stay committed to them.

Goals to 2030

  • Infrastructure Core water data systems running on resilient, cloud-ready, secure platforms with clear governance.
  • Open Data 100% of priority datasets open by default and discoverable through a single catalog.
  • Decisions Self-service analytics and dashboards supporting every key water program decision.
  • Monitoring SWAMP and Water Quality Monitoring Council data integrated and surfaced through My Water Quality.
  • People A skilled, collaborative data workforce sustained by a healthy workplace culture.

Roadmap

2026 — Foundation

Modernize core platforms, establish data governance, and strengthen SWAMP and monitoring data pipelines.

2027–2028 — Acceleration

Scale open data publishing and self-service analytics, integrate monitoring data through My Water Quality, and grow data skills.

2029–2030 — Realization

Data grounds every key decision as the infrastructure, monitoring, and people pillars deliver measurable impact against our 2030 goals.
